# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Payment methods are the ways customers execute payments for goods or services. Recurly supports a broad range of payment methods to help businesses accommodate customer preferences across geographies and payment ecosystems. For gateway-specific currency details, see the <a href="/guides/payment-orchestration/gateway-configuration-and-features/currency-support-by-gateway#/" target="_blank">currency support page</a> or the <a href="https://docs.recurly.com/recurly-subscriptions/docs/payment-gateways-1#/" target="_blank">payment gateways overview</a>.</div>
