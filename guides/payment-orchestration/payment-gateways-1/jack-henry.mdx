# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Jack Henry is a payment gateway designed specifically to accept ACH (Automated Clearing House) payments. To process ACH payments through Recurly, merchants must establish a Jack Henry account and complete onboarding with Recurly.</div>

# Configure Jack Henry in Recurly

<div className="rp-steps">
  <div className="rp-step">
    <div className="rp-step-num">1</div>
    <div><h4>Open Payment Gateways</h4><p>In Recurly, navigate to the <strong>Payment Gateways</strong> page and click <strong>Add a New Gateway</strong> at the top right.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div className="rp-step">
    <div className="rp-step-num">2</div>
    <div><h4>Select Jack Henry</h4><p>Choose <strong>Jack Henry</strong> from the list of available gateways.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div className="rp-step">
    <div className="rp-step-num">3</div>
    <div><h4>Enter your credentials</h4><p>Fill in the required fields with your Jack Henry account details: <strong>Entity ID</strong>, <strong>Store ID</strong>, <strong>Store Key</strong>, and <strong>Location ID</strong>.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div className="rp-step">
    <div className="rp-step-num">4</div>
    <div><h4>Enable Jack Henry</h4><p>Set the gateway to <strong>Enabled</strong> for new transactions when you're ready to go live.</p></div>
  </div>
  <div className="rp-step">
    <div className="rp-step-num">5</div>
    <div><h4>Save the gateway</h4><p>Click <strong>Add Payment Gateway</strong> to complete setup.</p></div>
  </div>
</div>
