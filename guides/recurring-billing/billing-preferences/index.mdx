# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Billing preferences are the settings that define how and when customers are charged — covering billing frequency, calendar alignment, shipping, billing info verification, and payment authorization. Configuring these correctly ensures your billing process runs accurately and transparently for both your business and your customers.</div>
