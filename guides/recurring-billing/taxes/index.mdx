# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Taxes are mandatory charges applied to goods, services, and income — and in global ecommerce, staying compliant across jurisdictions is non-trivial. Recurly's tax tools calculate, apply, and manage taxes automatically, so your billing stays accurate and compliant whether you're selling locally or internationally.</div>
