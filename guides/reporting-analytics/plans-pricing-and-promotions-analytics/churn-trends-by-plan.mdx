# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Plan Performance Subscription Churn is a feature within Recurly that gives you a detailed visualization of churn data through three line charts: total churn, voluntary churn, and involuntary churn.</div>

<div className="rp-callout rp-callout-note">
  <div><strong><i className="fa-solid fa-circle-info" aria-hidden="true"></i> Note</strong>It's important to understand the difference between a subscriber and a subscription. A single subscriber might hold multiple active subscriptions, but this dashboard counts each individual subscriber — not the number of subscriptions they hold. Data only includes plans that have had at least one subscriber.</div>
</div>

# Key details

The Churn view shows three line charts that track total, voluntary, and involuntary churn. Reviewing all three together helps you understand the reasons behind churn for each plan, which is essential for improving retention.
