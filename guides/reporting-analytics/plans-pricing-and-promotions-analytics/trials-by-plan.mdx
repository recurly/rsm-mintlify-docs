# Key details

The Trials view includes two swimlane charts and a status chart in the Subscription Trials section, plus a line chart in the Trial Conversion Rate section.

## Subscription trials

The swimlane charts show the status of trial subscriptions, including conversions to paid subscriptions and non-conversions due to failed payment or cancellation.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/8a0175d566132e3a354c153643acdd3119ae9a7c45c2ccde8fdea0dfe946a3f5-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />



<Image src="https://files.readme.io/c55f300cba052d74b40846a43a90f34706dad6dec5a8078ab7b383b22f297265-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


The status chart tracks the current state of subscriptions that began as trials, giving you insight into how many convert to full subscriptions and, when they don't, why.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/4daf0e242665c04bc5916f7ffb0baa63b445d10d5900afdf124570a0208916a4-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


## Trial conversion rate

This line chart shows the trial conversion rate for each plan, helping you assess how effective your trials are at turning users into paid subscribers.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/b2aa1e497d678c84952e31b1825d2198087734af96121e6d471a34691e5547f5-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />
