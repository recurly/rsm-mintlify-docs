# Key details

## Plan movement trend

Monthly net subscriber movement per plan (inbound minus outbound). Positive values mean a plan gained more subscribers than it lost from plan changes; negative values mean more subscribers moved away than arrived.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/1d7689ed1a8d9d4b37a4f1c9e7296009094d93749a944684925565b29580759b-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


## Incoming movement

Monthly count of subscribers who moved into each plan from another plan. Shows which plans are the most common destinations when subscribers change plans.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/4c76138ac0a7dae0e557514e4dcc184db2e42b8f7754d1848825ed1edc65cd4b-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


## Outgoing movement

Monthly count of subscribers who moved out of each plan to another plan. Shows which plans subscribers are most commonly leaving when switching plans.
