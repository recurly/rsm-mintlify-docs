# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">The sign-up decline rate dashboard tracks sign-up transactions that fail at the point of purchase. It helps you evaluate how well your payment method and gateway strategy is performing so you can work to lower your decline rate over time.</div>

# Key details

## General filters

<ul className="rp-list">
  <li><strong>Invoice creation date</strong>: Choose a specific timeframe, up to the last 18 months, using the start and end date dropdowns.</li>
  <li><strong>Gateway</strong>: View all payment gateways together or examine one at a time to see where issues are happening.</li>
  <li><strong>Failure type</strong>: Shows the top 5 reasons payments fail by default; adjust the filter to see more reasons.</li>
  <li><strong>Payment type</strong>: Break down failed payments by the payment method your customers use.</li>
  <li><strong>Sign-up decline type</strong>: Choose between sign-up declines or trial conversion declines.</li>
  <li><strong>Metric type</strong>: Visualize sign-up declines by transaction count or by total amount.</li>
</ul>

## Gateways and payment methods summary

This section shows how your gateways and payment methods perform at the point of sign-up, summarizing the decline rate for each gateway, supported payment method, and card brand.

<ul className="rp-list">
  <li><strong>Transactions attempted</strong>: The total number of attempts made through each gateway and payment method during the selected timeframe.</li>
  <li><strong>Transactions failed</strong>: How many of those attempted transactions were unsuccessful.</li>
  <li><strong>Sign-up decline rate overall</strong>: The percentage of transactions declined at sign-up, for a broad view of performance.</li>
  <li><strong>Sign-up decline rate, start to end of range</strong>: How the decline rate changed from the beginning to the end of your selected period.</li>
  <li><strong>Rate change</strong>: The percentage change in the sign-up decline rate over the chosen date range, useful for spotting trends or the effects of changes you've made.</li>
</ul>
