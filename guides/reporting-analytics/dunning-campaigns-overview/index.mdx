# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Dunning is the automated process Recurly uses to recover revenue from failed or declined transactions. This section brings together the reporting tools you need to monitor recovery performance, compare campaign strategies, and understand how dunning affects your subscription and revenue metrics.</div>
