# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Churn management analytics help you understand and reduce customer turnover. By looking at renewal rates, payment declines, and recovered revenue, these dashboards give you a complete view of churn, so you can catch failed payments before they cost you and pinpoint exactly where your processes need improvement.</div>
