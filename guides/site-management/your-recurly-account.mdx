# Definition

<div className="rp-definition">Your Recurly account is the primary interface for managing your engagement with the Recurly service. From here you can review your plan details, update billing information and company address, transition between plans, manage invoices, and close your site.</div>

# Key details

## Recurly subscription

This section shows a complete description of your plan — including the plan name (Starter, Pro, or Elite), any add-ons and their costs, the next invoice date, and your billing contact.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/5280a3e652f2590f7e848ea75aa4f35be2c1911c27960a69662b52aaee673332-image.png" align="center" width="40%" border={true} />


***

## Billing information

This section displays your primary billing details — name, the credit or debit card used to pay your plan, the card's expiration date, and the associated billing address.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/fca65a2-image.png" align="center" width="40%" border={true} />


To update your billing details, click **Edit**, fill in the required information, and click **Update Billing Info**.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/1d4aa2b-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />


***

## Company address

This section displays your company's address. Click the **Edit** button to update it.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/531dee5-image.png" align="center" width="40%" border={true} />


***

## Current plan and upgrading

The Current Plan section displays your active plan. Below the plan cost, an **Upgrade Available** button indicates that additional Recurly features are accessible. If the button isn't visible, you're likely already on an Elite plan. If you think this is an error, contact us.


<Image src="https://files.readme.io/a31edfdc761ed82218039faceed6ee174e156cb458cefa1a70c6953bde010de5-image.png" align="center" width="75%" border={true} />
